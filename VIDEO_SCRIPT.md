# GhostWhale AI: Autonomous Alpha Network — Video Script

A storyboard and voiceover transcript for a 3-minute presentation video.

---

### Segment 1: The Hook & Introduction (0:00 – 0:30)

* **Visual**: Camera starts on the React cyberpunk dashboard loading up. The neon cyan border glows, the circular holographic radar starts rotating, and status telemetry numbers (PnL, Win Rate) animate upwards.
* **Audio (Voiceover)**:
  > "Welcome to the future of decentralized intelligence. This is GhostWhale AI: Autonomous Alpha Network.
  > 
  > Most blockchain whale trackers are passive—they show you what happened hours ago. GhostWhale AI is an institutional-grade autonomous platform that tracks smart money on the Mantle network, submits it to a Multi-Agent Council for consensus debate, executes trades, and audits every single decision on-chain."

### Segment 2: Live Ingestion & Cyberpunk HUD (0:30 – 1:15)

* **Visual**: Close-up of the **Live Whale Feed** in the center and the **Liquidity Scanner** on the right side. A transaction flashes green indicating a large token transfer.
* **Audio (Voiceover)**:
  > "Under the hood, our Python-based scanner monitors Mantle block telemetry. It catches large ERC-20 transfers and native MNT moves exceeding fifty thousand dollars.

  > Simultaneously, our Liquidity Scanner watches Merchant Moe and Agni Finance pools for Mint and Burn events.

  > Everything is streamed in real time to our custom cyberpunk interface. We've built interactive controls like Threat Mode—which flashes warning indicators during extreme market volatility—and Stealth Mode for low-latency running."

### Segment 3: The Multi-Agent Council Debate (1:15 – 2:00)

* **Visual**: Zoom in on the **Multi-Agent Council** panel. The screen shows the 5 agents: WhaleHunter, LiquidityAI, MomentumAI, RiskGuard, and MacroAI. Each agent's vote (BUY/SELL/HOLD) and reasoning string fade onto the screen.
* **Audio (Voiceover)**:
  > "GhostWhale AI eliminates single-point-of-failure reasoning. When a whale movement is captured, our Multi-Agent Council goes to work.
  > 
  > Five specialized agents evaluate the data: WhaleHunter checks wallet tiers, Liquidity scans pool depth, Momentum charts price trends, RiskGuard filters wash trading, and Macro gauges gas costs.
  > 
  > The Orchestrator combines their votes through a weighted consensus formula. Only signals exceeding a sixty-five percent confidence threshold are registered for execution. No black boxes. Every reasoning step is formatted in plain English."

### Segment 4: Smart Contracts & ERC-8004 Identity (2:00 – 2:45)

* **Visual**: Split-screen showing the Solidity code files (SignalRegistry.sol, AgentIdentity.sol) on the left and the deployment script outputs in the terminal on the right.

* **Visual**: Camera pans out to show the full 3-column cyberpunk console operating smoothly, with green trade logs filling the ledger.
